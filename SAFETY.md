# Safety

Steady is a free, experimental wellness and music tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Evidence for binaural beats is limited and mixed. Do not use while driving or when you need to stay alert. Keep the volume low. If you have epilepsy, hearing problems, or a mental-health or substance-use condition, talk to a professional. In a crisis, contact emergency services or a crisis line.

## Don't use it
- While driving, cycling, operating machinery, or anywhere you need to hear alarms.
- Instead of real help when you need it.

## Ask a clinician first
Epilepsy; severe tinnitus, sound sensitivity or hearing loss in one ear; psychosis, dissociation, or PTSD with dissociation; anyone under 18.

## What the app does to keep you safe
- Loudness normalized to about −20 LUFS and a true-peak limiter at −1 dBTP (tested).
- Volume calibration with a reference sound; points to OS volume limiters.
- Default session lengths of 30 minutes or less; sleep sessions fade out and never loop all night.
- Weekly listening log using a conservative assumed level (the browser can't measure real loudness).
- No flashing or flickering visuals anywhere.
- Help lines (US: 988, SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357; directory for other countries) one tap away. Edit `src/data/helplines.ts` to add regions.
- Nudges toward professional support if the urge flow is used more than 5 times in a week, or the calm-down flow on 12+ of the last 14 days.

## Stop rules (from the research's self-experiment protocol)
- **Stop for the day** if you get a headache, dizziness, nausea, ringing or muffled hearing, rising panic or anger, or feel detached.
- **Stop the experiment and get help** if you have a seizure-like event, ringing in the ears lasting more than 24 hours, mood getting worse over a week, more craving or use, or you notice you're using this instead of real support.
